# Daily Digest Preferences

## Telegram
- Keep the Telegram version tight: weather/logistics first, then 3-5 bullets.
- Do not send generic encouragement, filler, or long article lists.
- Surface action over information. If there is no action, say so plainly.
- Telegram must never be a source-count report. It should read like a polished morning note and include the Notion link.
- Do not expose source warnings, stack traces, or raw technical errors in Telegram.

## Notion Format
- Use a clean executive structure: Good Morning Matt, then Key Events & Upcoming, Email Summary & Action Items, News Summary, Research Findings, Health & Recovery, Other / Admin, and Source Notes.
- The Notion page title already names the digest; avoid a duplicate visible title block.
- Use clean markdown links with short titles. Do not paste long bare URLs when a link title is available.
- Source Notes should be short and plain-English only.

## Priorities
- Highest priority: family logistics, calendar conflicts, overdue/high-priority tasks, health/medical/admin items, travel/reservations, finance/bills/taxes, and anything time-sensitive today.
- Important interests: AI, healthcare/business, local Charleston/Mt Pleasant signal, longevity/HRV/sleep/Zone 2, faith/family, investing/macro context, and notable Kentucky sports.
- Prefer "what matters / why / next move" over summaries.

## WHOOP
- Include WHOOP daily when available.
- If recovery is red for 2+ days, say it plainly and recommend a recovery-focused day.
- Tie guidance to HRV, RHR, sleep performance, strain, and rolling trends.
- Keep it practical: hydration, easy Zone 1/2 movement, sunlight, food, lower alcohol, and protecting sleep.
- Do not sound clinical or alarmist. Do not give medical advice.

## Inbox
- Skip low-value promotions, newsletters, and shopping unless they affect travel, school, finance, events, health, or family logistics.
- Highlight unread or time-sensitive email first.
- Group similar low-priority items instead of listing them one by one.

## Tasks
- Put overdue tasks before today's tasks.
- Keep high-priority tasks visible.
- Group normal/low-priority tasks when there are too many.

## News
- Be selective. Include fewer, better links.
- Local Charleston items should be actionable, family-relevant, business-relevant, or unusually interesting.
- Market/investing items should be macro-significant, not routine noise.
